Interpretation

For the entered assumptions, plan the displayed number as the initial estimate. Round up to whole animals.

Results are approximate normal-theory estimates. Small samples, non-normal outcomes, bounded data, count data, clustered housing, and complex designs may require specialist methods or software.

How to use it responsibly

  1. Specify the primary endpoint. Avoid powering a study for many outcomes without a multiplicity plan.
  2. Choose a meaningful effect. Use prior studies, pilot evidence, or a scientific/clinical justification.
  3. Estimate variability. Use an SD from a comparable model and measurement time point.
  4. Set alpha and power. Common planning values are α = 0.05 and power = 80–90%, but the choice must be justified.
  5. Adjust for attrition carefully. Use expected, evidence-based losses and explain them in the protocol.
  6. Review with the statistician and IAEC. Follow institutional, CPCSEA, and applicable legal requirements.

Design-specific notes

Independent continuous groups

Approximate two-group comparison of means. Assumes independent observations, common SD, a continuous outcome, and a prespecified difference. Unequal allocation is approximated using the selected ratio.

Paired / repeated measurements

Uses the SD of within-animal differences. It is suitable only when observations are genuinely paired. Repeated-measures designs with multiple time points may need a model-based calculation.

Binary outcomes

Approximate two-proportion comparison. For rare events, very small samples, or complete separation, exact or simulation-based methods may be preferable.

Correlation

Uses Fisher's z transformation to estimate the sample size for testing an expected correlation against a null correlation.

References and reporting checklist

  • ARRIVE Guidelines 2.0 — report study design, sample-size rationale, inclusion/exclusion criteria, randomization, blinding, outcome measures, and statistical methods.
  • NC3Rs Experimental Design Assistant (EDA) — supports planning animal experiments and identifying design issues.
  • CPCSEA / institutional IAEC guidance — check current applicable requirements for animal experimentation in India before protocol submission.
